Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

📌 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end exploratory data analysis of the Superstore dataset using Python and Pandas.

The main objective was to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, discount strategies, and business trends, and translate the findings into actionable business insights.

🎯 Business Questions

* How have sales and profit changed over the years?
* Which product categories and sub-categories are the most profitable?
* Which products generate losses?
* How does discounting affect profitability?
* Which customer segments generate the highest value?
* What seasonal patterns can be observed in sales and profit?

📊 Key Findings

📈 Business Growth

Sales increased from approximately $484K in 2014 to $733K in 2017.

Total profit also increased from approximately $49.5K to $93.4K over the same period.

🏆 Best Performing Category

Technology generated the highest total profit:

* Sales: $836.2K
* Profit: $145.5K
* Profit Margin: 17.4%

⚠️ Furniture Profitability

Furniture generated approximately $742K in sales, but its profit margin was only 2.49%.

The main loss-making sub-categories were:

* Tables: -$17.7K
* Bookcases: -$3.5K
* Supplies: -$1.2K

💸 Discount Impact

Higher discount levels were associated with substantially lower profit margins in the dataset.

At discount levels of 30% and above, profit margins became negative across the observed discount groups.

This indicates that high-discount strategies should be carefully evaluated based on product profitability and pricing structure.

👥 Customer Analysis

The analysis compared customer segments based on total sales, total profit, number of customers, and profit per customer to identify higher-value customer groups.

💡 Business Recommendations

1. Review discount strategies, especially high-discount orders.
2. Investigate pricing and cost structures for loss-making Furniture products.
3. Focus on profitable product categories such as Technology.
4. Monitor both revenue growth and profit margin when evaluating business performance.
5. Use customer profitability metrics to support customer retention and marketing strategies.
